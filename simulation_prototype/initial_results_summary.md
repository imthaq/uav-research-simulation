# Initial Results Summary

All 9 scenarios (baseline, false_positive, false_negative, sensor_noise, latency, sensor_dropout, confidence_error, naive_fusion, trust_weighted_fusion) were run against the default 4-UAV swarm. The numbers below are averages across 5 seeded runs per scenario, taken from results/results_summary.csv (generated by metrics_analysis.py --runs 5). Per-run raw logs (3 runs per scenario) are also saved individually under logs/ via run_experiments.py, for step-by-step inspection.

## Scenarios tested

1. baseline - no perception error, no fusion
2. false_positive - phantom detections only (rate 0.08)
3. false_negative - missed detections only (rate 0.25)
4. sensor_noise - Gaussian position noise on real detections (std 1.5)
5. latency - detections delayed 5 steps before reaching the controller
6. sensor_dropout - periodic total sensor blackouts (prob 0.02, duration 8 steps)
7. confidence_error - reported detection confidence miscalibrated (level 0.35), no fusion to compensate
8. naive_fusion - combined error profile (20% false negative, noise 1.5, confidence error 0.15), unweighted cross-UAV fusion
9. trust_weighted_fusion - same combined error profile as naive_fusion, but fusion weights each UAV's contribution by its reported confidence

## Number of runs

45 total: 5 seeded runs per scenario across 9 scenarios (metrics_analysis.py --runs 5, seeds 42-46). A separate 3-run pass (run_experiments.py --runs 3) saves the full per-step CSV log for each individual run under logs/.

## Metrics collected

Every run's per-step CSV log (logs/<scenario>_run<n>.csv) records, per UAV per step: position, goal position, actual vs. perceived obstacle position, perception error type, confidence value, fusion mode, action taken, distance to nearest UAV, distance to obstacle, collision risk flag, unnecessary avoidance flag, missed response flag, and mission completed flag (full column list in simulation_readme.md).

The aggregated results_summary.csv (one row per scenario per run) records: scenario, run number, fusion mode, false positive rate, false negative rate, noise level, latency steps, dropout probability, confidence error level, collision risk count, unnecessary avoidance count, missed response count, fusion recovery count, mission success, average response time, total near misses, and average formation error.

Average confidence error (mean absolute difference between a detection's true and reported confidence) is also calculated per run and printed by metrics_analysis.py, for scenarios where confidence_error_level is greater than 0. It is 0.000 for every scenario except confidence_error (0.250) and the two fusion scenarios (0.119), matching each scenario's configured confidence_error_level.

## Baseline result

0 of 5 baseline runs succeeded. Baseline produced zero collisions, zero near misses, and zero unnecessary avoidance - the swarm never encounters or reacts to a perception error, since none is injected. The failure is purely a formation/steering issue: only 2 of 4 UAVs reach their goal slot within the 600-step limit, because UAVs converging on the goal area treat each other as obstacles and orbit rather than settle. Average formation error is 9.497, identical across all 5 baseline runs since there is no randomness in a zero-error scenario.

## False positive result

0 of 5 runs succeeded. Phantom detections caused an average of 9.8 unnecessary avoidance actions per run, with no collision risk or missed response impact (as expected, since a phantom is an extra detection, not a missing one). The effect is mild compared to the noise and dropout scenarios.

## False negative result

0 of 5 runs succeeded. Missing 25% of real detections produced an average of 6.4 collision-risk events and 1.0 missed responses per run - a UAV that never perceives an approaching obstacle at all cannot react to it.

## Latency result

0 of 5 runs succeeded. A 5-step detection delay produced zero collision risk, zero near misses, and zero missed responses in this configuration - the swarm's speed and the sensor range give it enough margin to still react in time despite the delay. Average formation error (10.702) is the highest of any scenario, suggesting the delay does distort formation-keeping even though it doesn't create direct safety incidents at these settings.

## Dropout result

0 of 5 runs succeeded. Periodic total sensor blackouts produced an average of 15.6 collision-risk events and 6.2 missed responses per run - the second-worst missed-response rate after sensor_noise's indirect effects, since a UAV in a blackout has no way to react to a real threat until the blackout ends.

## Which error caused the worst behavior

Sensor noise was the most disruptive by a wide margin: an average of 56.0 collision-risk events and 187.4 unnecessary avoidance actions per run, both the highest of any scenario. Noisy position estimates repeatedly place the perceived obstacle in the wrong spot, so UAVs both crowd too close to the real obstacle and steer away from empty space. Sensor dropout was the next most disruptive, with the highest missed-response count (6.2) since a blacked-out UAV has no fallback estimate to act on.

## Does fusion help

Fusion recovers a large share of individually-missed detections. Both naive_fusion and trust_weighted_fusion ran a harsher combined error profile (20% false-negative rate plus noise plus confidence miscalibration) than any single-error scenario above, and still averaged 65.0 and 68.6 fusion-recovered detections per run, holding missed response at 0.0 and collision risk down to 0.2 - far better than what sensor_noise or sensor_dropout alone produced with a lighter error profile. The trade-off is more unnecessary avoidance (78.6 and 83.8 per run): sharing more, sometimes still-noisy, detections across the swarm means UAVs react to weaker or farther signals more often, even when nothing is actually close. Trust-weighted fusion did not clearly outperform naive fusion at these error levels (83.8 vs 78.6 unnecessary avoidance, 68.6 vs 65.0 fusion recovery) - with 5 runs per scenario the difference is within noise, and a larger --runs sample would be needed to say more.

## What needs improvement in the prototype

No scenario reaches mission_success (all UAVs reach goal and zero collisions), including baseline. UAVs consistently treat each other as obstacles near the goal formation and orbit rather than settle, capping uavs_reached_goal at 2/4 even with zero perception error. This is the single biggest gap: retuning goal_gain vs. avoidance_gain, widening goal_tolerance, or adding per-UAV goal-slot yielding logic would likely let more of the swarm actually finish, and is a prerequisite for any of the perception-error scenarios showing a real mission_success signal.

Sensor dropout has no memory of the last known obstacle position once a blackout starts; carrying forward a decaying-confidence "last known" estimate during a blackout would likely reduce its missed-response count.

Sensor noise has no filtering - each noisy reading is acted on directly. A simple moving-average or Kalman-style filter on the perceived obstacle position would likely cut both its collision-risk and unnecessary-avoidance counts.

With 5 runs per scenario, results (especially the naive vs. trust-weighted fusion comparison) are still somewhat noisy; metrics_analysis.py --runs 10 or higher would give a steadier read on which fusion strategy is actually better.
