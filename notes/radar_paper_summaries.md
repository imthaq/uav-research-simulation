 
	Summary of the radar detection based 
				Research papers 
###1: A Lightweight CNN-Based Method for Micro-Doppler Feature-Based UAV Detection and Classification	

This 2025 paper introduces a compact neural network utilizing coordinate attention mechanisms on Range-Doppler maps generated by an 8.75 GHz FMCW radar. By effectively focusing on specific micro-Doppler signatures, the system achieves over 98% accuracy in differentiating drones from other moving objects. Its lightweight footprint is ideal for real-time edge processing, though it may require retraining for highly cluttered environments unseen in its dataset.

2: High-Resolution FMCW Radar for Small UAVDetection Using GNU Software-Defined Radio	

This research details a practical, SDR-based 2.45 GHz FMCW radar system designed specifically to detect low-RCS (0.01 m²) drones like the DJI Phantom. Using Doppler Signal-to-Clutter Ratio (DSCR) filtering and GPU acceleration, the authors achieved a 300-meter detection range in real-world cluttered environments. The modular architecture proves highly cost-effective, though the reliance on the 2.45 GHz ISM band slightly limits its interference resilience

3: Small Drone Detection Using Hybrid Beamforming 24 GHz Fully Integrated CMOS Radar	

This study presents a fully integrated 24 GHz mmWave radar on a 65 nm CMOS chip, leveraging hybrid analog beamforming for 3D drone detection. The system balances ultra-low power consumption (under 400 mW) with effective spatial scanning across elevation and azimuth planes. While its transmission power and range resolution are somewhat constrained compared to 77 GHz systems, it offers a highly scalable hardware solution for distributed anti-drone arrays.

4: LiDAR Technology for UAV Detection: From Fundamentals and Operational Principles to Advanced Detection and Classification Techniques	

This LiDAR technology is very effective in providing 3D high resolution images using light rays technology while measuring the distance also. Moreover not only this when combined with different CNN models or YOLOv(x) where x = 1,2…n  models we are accurately able to distinguish between different objects.

5: Convolutional Neural Network-Based Drone Detection and Classification Using Overlaid Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave (FMCW) Range–Doppler Images	

In general if i have to say they use range doppler map image method to combine multiple  images of the object or drone into one then feed it to the multiple models of SVM and CNN with Googlenet a type of CNN model providing the most accurate result it was 96% accurate without range Doppler map imagining but inclusion of this method increased the accuracy to 99.6% percent making to more reliable.

6: Micro-Doppler Signature Detection and Recognition of UAVs Based on OMP Algorithm	

The paper discuss about identifying different UAVs based on the speed of their fans and frequency difference obtained by abs(Rays transmitted - rays Obtained). This frequency signal is used to extract all the other properties required by the models such as Micro-Doppler Echo Model.


7: Radar–Camera Fusion in Perspective View and Bird’s Eye View for 3D Object Detection	

This research provides a sophisticated framework for merging mmWave radar data with optical camera feeds by projecting radar cross-section (RCS) and depth data into the camera's perspective view. Using an attention mechanism, the system dynamically weights the most reliable sensor data to construct a highly accurate Bird's Eye View (BEV) map of surrounding objects. While designed for broad 3D object detection, its methodology for overcoming visual depth limitations using mmWave radar is directly applicable to UAV swarm obstacle avoidance and navigation.

8: UAV Detection with Passive Radar: Algorithms, Applications, and Challenges	 

In general Passive radar are not only advantageous against active radars, but its several different types proved to be more advantageous than other radar based systems such as MicroDropple radar , FMCW radar , COTS mmWaver radar system. Moreover if we combine this radar with UAV trajectory tracking , image mapping in 2D space , using range doppler image mapper and deep learning  to distinguish between birds and drones it becomes most efficient and serves its purpose pretty well.


9: Small UAV Target Detection Algorithm Using the YOLOv8n-RFL Based on Radar Detection Technology	

In summary the main aim of the research is to be able to detect small, low altitude UAVs while also considering different environment factors such as radar clutter and noise. The proposed solution was to incorporate the YOLOv8 model combined with the RD(Range Doppler) planner graphs on which our model will be trained. Moreover a different model was used to compare the results where we achieved high precision rates using YOLOv8 whereas the results were closer to the result of YOLO but less. Therefore YOLOv8 not only has shown exceptional performance but can be considered for implementation in future projects as well.
